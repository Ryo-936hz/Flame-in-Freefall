# Saffire configuration contrast

Use Saffire I and III to illustrate the difference between a large fabric sample in the Cygnus flight duct and the small opposed-flow PMMA slab tests in the ISS BASS-II series. Saffire II offers a mixed-material sample card, with two smaller SIBAL fabric samples and additional silicone and PMMA configurations. Sample width, material, flow direction and confinement all matter to this comparison. The study authors describe the Saffire I/III fabric as 94 cm long by 40.6 cm wide and the Saffire II samples as 29 cm by 5 cm. [Urban et al., *Flame spread: Effects of microgravity and scale*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.combustflame.2018.10.012) · [NASA, *Results of Large-Scale Spacecraft Flammability Tests*](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20170008805)

PSI-98's thickness column is headed `Sample Thickness (cm)` and contains `0.37`. PSI-100 contains `.33 cm` in its thickness cells. The 2014 ICES hardware paper describes approximately 0.32 mm for the material chosen for I/III. These sources use different stated scales. The CSV text and report description stay separate until the measurement and units are resolved. [Saffire hardware development paper](https://backend.orbit.dtu.dk/ws/portalfiles/portal/97737980/2014_ICES_SFS_Demo_Paper_265_5_16_2014.pdf)

For PSI-99, the literal heading `?-g Spread Length` includes values with units of mm/s, while the separate `?-g Burn Length` column includes lengths. The 1-g columns mix quantities and labels such as `Acceleratory`. Leave `037 mm` as written and retain blank material cells as source absences. Lines 13 and 16 describe, respectively, a derived oxygen value and regression of an anchored flame. A quantitative import needs a metric definition distinguishing spread rate, burned length, regression and ground observations, as well as reconciled sample and flow identifiers.

Saffire remains documentary evidence in this increment. Its local entries support a configuration contrast, with their discrepancies visible. Numeric comparisons with BASS-II require a separate review of source versions, measurement definitions and physical comparability. NASA PSI publicly lists `CC0-1.0` for PSI-98.
